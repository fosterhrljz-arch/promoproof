# Reporting interface research for PromoProof 2.1

Reviewed public primary documentation on 17 November 2026. No external accounts, private dashboards or customer records were accessed. These observations inform PromoProof's interaction principles; its interface is independently designed.

## User-selected public references

The user explicitly selected these pages in place of the previously mentioned prototype and redesign brief. Public text was retrieved for all three. Shopify's rendered page redirected to its Chinese locale; its English source text was also read. Shopify and Square landing-page visuals were inspected in the in-app browser. Lightspeed's layout observations below are grounded in its published navigation article; its embedded dashboard image did not complete browser rendering during this final pass.

| Reference | Observed design and behavior | Independent design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shopify Analytics](https://www.shopify.com/analytics) | A large benefit-led introduction precedes product chart examples. The examples pair a leading number with a restrained chart, while the product description supports overview-to-detail exploration and configurable reports. | Explain the promotion decision before showing inputs. Use a real worked example on Home and pair the headline financial results with a sensitivity chart in Analysis. |
| [Square Analytics](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/features/dashboard/analytics) | The landing page uses generous white space, strong dark headings and blue calls to action. Its content progresses from a business benefit to report capabilities, period comparisons, CSV exports and access on different devices. | Use a neutral canvas, blue primary actions and short practical explanations. Give comparison, export and actual-result review visible destinations while keeping the opening page simple. |
| [Navigating Lightspeed Analytics](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410640218523-Navigating-Lightspeed-Analytics) | The article describes a persistent left menu, top-level dashboard sections, a collapsible glossary, shared location context and drillable visualizations with export actions. | Separate global navigation from Decision / Compare scenarios / Review actuals. Keep the product, period and goal in context, and place detailed definitions and calculations behind clear disclosures. |

These are public product and help pages, not hands-on audits of authenticated vendor dashboards. No vendor imagery is included in the application or delivery.

## Supporting interaction documentation

| Product and primary source | Observed interaction | Application in PromoProof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shopify Analytics: overview dashboard](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/overview-dashboard/using-the-overview-dashboard) | Summary metric cards lead to related detailed reports. Date and comparison controls apply across the dashboard. Numerical changes and charts work together. | Put the promotion decision and four key financial indicators first. Clicking an indicator reveals the financial details. Keep one shared product, period and goal across scenarios. |
| [Lightspeed Retail X: retail dashboard](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/25533720653211-Using-the-retail-dashboard) | A summary dashboard combines performance widgets with detailed tables. “View report” carries the dashboard's selected filters into the report. | Keep scenario and business-goal context visible. Retain the selected comparison case and metric when opening an offer and returning to comparison. Put the full financial table below the decision and chart. |
| [Lightspeed Retail R: reports](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/228841987-About-reports) | Report families use consistent filtering, export and print actions. | Use a consistent Export action for CSV, JSON and Print/PDF, and retain a direct CSV action on actual-results reviews. |
| [Square Dashboard: summaries and reports](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5381-in-app-summaries-and-reports) | Distinct report views, configurable metrics and filters, comparisons, exports and nearby explanations help users interpret sales figures. Configuration is separated from the main report. | Separate Decision, Compare scenarios and Review actuals. Use progressive input disclosures, contextual explanations and a dedicated How It Works page. Label assumptions and entered actuals explicitly. |

The application choices in the last column are design inferences, not claims that these vendors use PromoProof's financial rules. No screenshots, interface assets or proprietary layout were copied.

## What the research does not imply

PromoProof remains a one-product contribution model, not a POS analytics suite. It has no connected sales feeds, demand forecasts, real-time dashboards or currency conversion. Inputs and actuals are entered by the user. Comparison cases are assumptions rather than probabilistic predictions. Existing v2 financial rules, rounding, inventory limits and frozen review calculations are preserved.

The five-product business-model research from v2 remains in [RESEARCH-v2.md](RESEARCH-v2.md); the canonical calculation specification is [FINANCIAL-MODEL-v2.md](FINANCIAL-MODEL-v2.md).
